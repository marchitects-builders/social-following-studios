## Pilot A — SaaS founder

**Source structure:** recurring-series frame + contrarian promise.  
**Source:** Lena Media — https://www.tiktok.com/@lenamed1a/video/7672508898892369166  
**Original concept:** Make one technical feature understandable in one sentence.

### Blueprint

- Hook type: corrective / clarity challenge
- Runtime target: 28–32 seconds
- Information order: problem → three-step rewrite → example → CTA
- Visual language: founder at desk, product UI cutaway, large six-word text overlays
- Scene changes: 0s, 4s, 10s, 18s, 26s
- Close: ask the viewer to test the sentence on someone outside the company

### Original spoken script

“If your SaaS takes a full minute to explain, the problem may not be the product. Try this. First, name the person who feels the pain. Second, name the job they cannot finish. Third, say what becomes easier after your tool. Not: ‘an AI-powered workflow platform.’ Say: ‘It turns scattered client requests into one approved plan.’ If a friend outside your industry understands it in five seconds, you have a usable hook.”

### On-screen text and shots

1. `YOUR SAAS ISN'T BORING. THE EXPLANATION IS.` — medium talking-head shot.
2. `WHO HURTS?` — quick punch-in.
3. `WHAT JOB IS STUCK?` — product UI crop with generic placeholder data.
4. `WHAT GETS EASIER?` — before/after diagram.
5. `TEST IT IN 5 SECONDS` — return to speaker.

### Render prompt shared across engines

Create a 9:16, approximately 30-second vertical marketing short using a fictional adult founder and a clearly fictional SaaS interface. Use the supplied spoken script verbatim. Clean studio-office setting, natural delivery, restrained hand movement, five deliberate cuts matching the shot list, and no brand logos or generated text. Add the approved captions and on-screen wording in post-production after the raw render passes QC. Do not invent customer metrics, testimonials, or product results. Keep the same fictional person, wardrobe, lighting, and voice throughout.

## Pilot B — course creator

**Source structure:** knowledge-gap hook + compact lesson.  
**Source:** seun | DFW Creator — https://www.tiktok.com/@seunseriki_/video/7673736438000749837  
**Original concept:** Replace a broad course promise with one observable learner outcome.

### Blueprint

- Hook type: overlooked lesson
- Runtime target: 26–30 seconds
- Information order: common mistake → test → rewritten example → CTA
- Visual language: educator beside a whiteboard, worksheet close-up, three highlighted phrases
- Scene changes: 0s, 5s, 12s, 20s, 26s
- Close: rewrite one module title using the test

### Original spoken script

“Here is the course-creation lesson most outlines miss: every module needs an observable finish line. ‘Understand your audience’ is not one. ‘Interview five buyers and sort their answers into three objections’ is. Use this test: can a learner show you what they made, changed, or decided? If not, the lesson is still too vague. Rewrite one module title as an action before you record another video.”

### On-screen text and shots

1. `YOUR MODULE NEEDS A FINISH LINE` — educator to camera.
2. `VAGUE: UNDERSTAND YOUR AUDIENCE` — whiteboard.
3. `VISIBLE: 5 INTERVIEWS → 3 OBJECTIONS` — worksheet close-up.
4. `MADE • CHANGED • DECIDED` — three-word kinetic overlay.
5. `REWRITE ONE MODULE` — educator closes notebook.

### Render prompt shared across engines

Create a 9:16, approximately 28-second educational short using a fictional adult course creator. Use the supplied script verbatim. Warm home-studio setting, credible teaching delivery, whiteboard and worksheet inserts, five cuts matching the shot list, and no generated text. Add accurate captions and approved worksheet text in post-production. No logos, testimonials, revenue claims, or real student data. Keep the same fictional person, voice, wardrobe, and lighting throughout.

## Pilot C — coach

**Source structure:** three-part framework tied to premium positioning.  
**Source:** aprilleunfiltered — https://www.tiktok.com/@aprilleunfiltered/video/7675019205867031822  
**Original concept:** Three proof layers a coach can publish without exaggerating results.

### Blueprint

- Hook type: myth correction
- Runtime target: 30–34 seconds
- Information order: myth → three proof layers → safety qualifier → CTA
- Visual language: coach to camera, annotated document inserts, simple numbered cards
- Scene changes: 0s, 5s, 11s, 17s, 24s, 30s
- Close: audit the last nine posts for the three layers

### Original spoken script

“A premium coaching offer does not need louder promises. It needs clearer proof. Publish these three layers. One: your method — how you make decisions. Two: your evidence — only verified examples you are allowed to share. Three: your standards — what you will not promise or shortcut. Together, they show expertise without pretending every client gets the same result. Audit your last nine posts. If one layer is missing, that is your next post.”

### On-screen text and shots

1. `PREMIUM ≠ LOUDER PROMISES` — direct-to-camera hook.
2. `1. METHOD` — annotated framework page.
3. `2. VERIFIED EVIDENCE` — redacted generic case note.
4. `3. STANDARDS` — checklist.
5. `NO GUARANTEED OUTCOMES` — brief safety card.
6. `AUDIT YOUR LAST 9 POSTS` — return to speaker.

### Render prompt shared across engines

Create a 9:16, approximately 32-second vertical short using a fictional adult business coach. Use the supplied script verbatim. Premium but restrained office setting, direct delivery with natural short-form energy, six cuts matching the shot list, generic document inserts, and no generated text. Add accurate captions and document wording in post-production. No real brand, client, likeness, testimonial, revenue number, or guaranteed outcome. Maintain one fictional person, voice, wardrobe, and lighting throughout.
